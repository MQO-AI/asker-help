## Start a Bulletin Board

Use a Bulletin Board to collect private activity responses and run moderated discussions over time.

1. [Create a Bulletin Board study](./sessions/bulletin-boards/create-a-bulletin-board) and [build the study flow](./sessions/bulletin-boards/build-the-study-flow).
2. [Enrol participants](./sessions/bulletin-boards/manage-study-people), [set the study window and publish the flow](./sessions/bulletin-boards/publish-open-and-close), then [send invitations](./sessions/bulletin-boards/send-study-email).
3. During the study window, select **Open study** when you’re ready for participants to begin.

If you have a personal invitation, see [Participate in a Bulletin Board study](./sessions/bulletin-boards/participate-in-a-study).

## Start a Focus Group

Use a Focus Group to run a live, moderated discussion with invited participants.

1. [Create a Session Group and Focus Group](./sessions/focus-groups/create-a-session-group), then [set the date and time](./sessions/focus-groups/schedule-a-focus-group).
2. [Assign people](./sessions/focus-groups/assign-people), [prepare your guide and materials](./sessions/focus-groups/prepare-guide-and-materials), and [send invitations](./sessions/focus-groups/send-invitations).
3. [Practise in a test room](./sessions/focus-groups/practise-as-moderator) before you [moderate the live session](./sessions/focus-groups/moderate-a-focus-group).

If you have a personal invitation, see [Join a Focus Group](./participant-help/focus-groups/join-a-focus-group).

If your organisation has given you access, [open Asker](https://app.asker.ca/). If you cannot access an area you expect to use, ask your organisation's Asker administrator to review your access.

If you received an invitation to take a survey or join a session, use the link in that invitation. The person or organisation that invited you can help with access to that specific study.
