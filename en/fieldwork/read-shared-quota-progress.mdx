A shared quota progress page shows aggregate progress for selected survey targets. Open the link a survey administrator supplied for your work, and keep it private.

Each row shows a status, a label, a target, completed responses, remaining responses, and achievement. Achievement is shown as a percentage of that row's target. For example, 42 completes against a target of 100 is 42.0% achievement. A percentage target can also show its equivalent number of responses beneath it.

The label beneath the quota name explains the scope:

- **Supplier allocation** identifies a quota configured for a supplier or survey connection.
- **Selected survey quota** shows the saved quota as selected by the administrator. It can include respondents from more than one source, so do not assume it represents only your allocation.

A row can show **Accepting**, **Places held**, **Target reached**, or **Counting only**. Counting-only quotas track progress without stopping respondents at the target. Ask the survey administrator how the quota applies to your fieldwork before changing your recruitment.

The page displays **Last updated** and refreshes while it is open and focused. Choose **Refresh** to request current figures. If the latest refresh fails, the page identifies the figures as the last available ones. If the link is unavailable, choose **Try again**. If it remains unavailable, ask the sender whether it has been revoked or replaced.
