A quota progress link gives an audience an aggregate view of selected quota rows. Survey administrators can create a separate link for each audience. Save the quota rows before creating a link, and select only the rows that audience needs to see.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

1. Open the survey's **Quotas** page and choose **Share**.
2. Choose **Create link**. Select a **Shared page title**: a custom title, the internal survey name, or the respondent-facing title. Enter a title if you choose **Custom title**.
3. Under **Visible quota rows**, select the saved rows. For each row, choose its scope:
   - **Selected survey quota** displays that quota's saved counting rule and target.
   - **Supplier allocation** is for a rule that requires a trusted supplier or survey connection.
4. Choose **Create share link**, then use **Preview shared page** to review the audience's view. Copy the link for the intended audience.

For example, create one link showing a supplier-specific allocation and another showing selected survey-wide quotas. Preview each link before sharing it with its intended audience.

Each row retains its own counting rule. Selecting a survey-wide quota does not turn its combined count into a supplier-specific count. The shared page shows the selected rows' status, target, completes and achievement percentage, and remaining amount.

To change an audience's view, open **Share** and choose **Edit rows and title**. To remove access, choose **Revoke link** and confirm. Other links remain active. If a selected quota has been deleted, remove the unavailable row before saving. A recipient with a revoked or invalid link sees that the quota link is unavailable.

For the recipient's view, see [Read a shared quota progress page](./read-shared-quota-progress).
