A moderator test session lets you practise room controls before the live Focus Group. You need a scheduled session and an active personal moderator link.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

1. Open the link and check your display name, microphone, camera, and preview. Choose **Audio and video** in the room if you need to change devices later.
2. Choose **Enter as test session** when it is offered. The room identifies itself as a test session.
3. Try the guide, prepared materials, screen sharing, focus controls, and **Pop-out controls**. Use **Test companion** in the test room to practise controls on another device.
4. Try starting and stopping a test recording and marking highlights. Review the practice summary, then choose **Leave test**.

The test stays separate from the scheduled session. Recording and highlights are simulated, so there is no recording file or transcript. Admission, messages, and saved session notes are available in the live session. Leaving a test does not end the scheduled session.

When you are ready to host, return to your personal link and enter the private preparation room. Choose **Start session and join main room** when the live discussion is ready to begin.
