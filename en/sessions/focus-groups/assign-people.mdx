Focus Group assignments identify the participants, moderators, and observers who can receive personal joining links. Open the Focus Group and choose **Prepare → People** before sending invitations.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

1. In **Moderators and observers**, add the person's name and email if they are not listed, then choose **Add**. Adding someone here assigns them to this Focus Group. For an existing person, select **This session** to assign them here or **All sessions** to assign them across sessions. Review any confirmation when changing an existing assignment, because it can replace joining links or send calendar cancellations. Changes save automatically.
2. In **Participants**, the list initially shows people assigned to this Focus Group. For someone already in the Session Group, choose **Add from Session Group**, find the person, and select **Assign to this Focus Group**. For someone new, choose **Add participant**, enter their name and optional email, then choose **Add to this Focus Group**. An email address is needed for an email invitation.
3. If you are viewing the Session Group list, choose **Show this Focus Group** to return to the assigned list. Check that the person appears there. Review any mix warning and the participant targets. Use **Copy invite** when you need a personal link, or use the invitation controls to review and send an email. Keep each link private and give it only to its intended recipient.

For example, assign a manually managed participant to this session, then review the person’s link and email before sending an invitation. Assignment alone does not send one.

If a participant has a **Manage booking** link, use Registration to change their booking or status. The controls in this roster are unavailable for that booking. A missing or inactive link may require an assignment or renewal. Check the message beside the person before trying again.

Assignment and sending are separate actions. See [Send Focus Group invitations](./send-invitations).
