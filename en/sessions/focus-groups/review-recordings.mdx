Recording Studio lets authorised staff review Focus Group recordings and transcripts, prepare versions, and create assets. After a moderator records a session, open **Results → Recording Studio**. If there are multiple segments, choose the **Recording segment** you want to work with. Check its status before sharing it.

## Review a transcript

Asker requests transcription automatically when a new recording is ready. Processing may take time or fail. Choose **Open transcript** to review its status and available text. When segments are available, choose **Download transcript**. If transcription fails, use **Retry transcription** when available.

Speakers are identified automatically when possible. Review the transcript and speaker labels before using them. The recording settings saved before capture determine the language and label choices for a new recording. See [Set shared Focus Group defaults](./set-shared-defaults).

## Prepare a recording version

1. When the recording is ready, choose **Recording versions** and open **Prepare a version**.
2. Choose the transcription language, speaker labels, name badges, and up to 20 available screener fields. Badge changes are available only when supported by the recording.
3. Choose **Prepare version**. When it is ready, use **Preview** to review it or select it for internal use. You can return to **Original recording** with **Select original**.

Selecting a version for internal use does not change existing shares. Choose a version separately for a new delivery in Sharing Centre. The **Download full recording** control in Studio downloads the original. In Sharing Centre, you can choose a ready version for download. If a version is unavailable or fails, review its status and prepare another version when appropriate.

## Make an asset

In **Asset Generator**, use an available recording with the original video. If the selected version uses a different video, choose **Select original** under **Recording versions**. Choose **Clips & GIFs** or **Freezeframe**. For a clip, drag the timeline ends, choose **Preview clip**, add a label, and choose **Save clip**. For a GIF, choose **GIF**, choose a point on the timeline, adjust its range, and choose **Create GIF**. GIFs are limited to 10 seconds. For a still, choose a point, adjust the frame within the displayed window, and choose **Capture freezeframe**.

The asset card shows its processing status. Wait until it is ready before sharing. Moderator highlights are prepared as clips for sharing when ready. If preparation fails, use **Retry clip** when available. If there are no recordings, use **Refresh recordings** after a segment has been recorded.
