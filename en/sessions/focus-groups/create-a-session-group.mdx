A Session Group organises related Focus Groups and their shared settings under a Project. You need access to Sessions and a Project for the group.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

1. Open **Focus Groups**, then choose **Manage Session Groups**. To start a new group, choose **New Session Group**.
2. Enter a **Session Group name**, select or create a Project, and choose **Create session group**. A Project can have more than one Session Group when you need different guides, staff, or recruitment.
3. In **Add your first session**, choose **New Session**. For an existing group, open it and choose **All sessions**, then **New session**.
4. Enter a **Session title**, select **Focus Group**, and choose **Create session**. The Focus Group workspace opens, ready for its schedule and people.

Use the Sessions list to find current or archived sessions. You can filter the list by format. To reuse a setup, choose **Clone** beside a Focus Group, review the new title and the copy details, and choose **Create draft copy**.

To update the group's name, status, time zone, study type, objective, target audience, or tags, open **Session Group settings** and choose **Save session group settings**. Manage the Project and its team from the linked Project overview.

The copy contains the guide, material selections, message templates, and room settings. It does not copy participants, bookings, recordings, or results. Set a new date and review the people assigned to it. Staff assigned to all Focus Groups receive new personal links, but no invitations are sent by cloning.

If a group or session is archived, restore it before preparing it again. If creation or loading fails, check your access and try again.
