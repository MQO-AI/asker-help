Shared Focus Group settings provide defaults, people, and resources for sessions in a Session Group. Open the group and choose **Shared Focus Group settings**.

## Defaults

In **Defaults**, set the session duration and target participant count. To configure an incentive, select **Incentives enabled** and enter the amount in whole dollars. Choose **Save defaults**. An individual group can use an incentive override in **Participant arrival**. Check the joining page before invitations are sent to confirm what participants will see.

The same page contains **Shared arrival defaults** and **Shared recording defaults**, each with its own save button. Arrival settings control the waiting room, observer greenroom, device requirements, and help text. See [Configure participant arrival](./configure-participant-arrival).

In **Shared recording defaults**, choose whether recordings include name badges, the transcription language, and whether speaker labels use names when known or numbers. You can select up to 20 authorised screener fields for the transcript. The structure preview contains no participant answers. Choose **Save recording defaults**. These settings apply to new recordings after saving, not existing recordings. Each Focus Group can override individual settings in **Prepare → Participant arrival**, then choose **Save recording settings**. Choose **Use shared setting** to remove an override and save again.

If a saved screener field becomes unavailable, it will not appear in new transcripts. Review the warning and any removal decision before saving. If the save cannot be confirmed or the settings changed elsewhere, load the latest settings, review your changes, and save again.

## Moderators and observers

In **Moderators and observers**, add each person by name and email. Choose **All Focus Groups** to assign them across the group, or **Select** and check the sessions they should join. Assignment gives a person a personal joining link; it does not send an invitation. Changes to these assignments save as you make them.

## Guide, materials, and invitations

Use **Guide** and **Materials** to prepare resources that individual groups can use. In **Invitation defaults**, choose the role and email template, edit the sender and reminder rules, and save. A Focus Group can use a session-specific guide, material selection, or invitation override. See [Prepare the guide and materials](./prepare-guide-and-materials) and [Send Focus Group invitations](./send-invitations).

The participant roster is for people recruited outside Registration. For a Registration-managed booking, use the linked Registration to manage the booking.
