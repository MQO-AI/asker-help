A Focus Group recording captures a live session segment for later review. A moderator starts recording manually from the main room while the session is live. Follow your organisation's consent process and tell the group before starting.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

1. In the moderator room, choose **Start recording**. Wait for the recording status to show that it is active. Participants and observers see a notice when recording starts.
2. While recording, choose **Mark highlight** and select a range for the last or next 30 seconds, one minute, or two minutes. For a future highlight, wait for the save confirmation after the selected interval ends before stopping the recording or leaving the room.
3. Choose **Stop recording**, review the confirmation, and choose **Stop recording** again. The captured segment is saved for Recording Studio.
4. If needed, choose **Start another recording** when the control becomes available after the previous segment stops. Earlier segments remain available.

The control shows when recording is starting, stopping, processing, or unavailable. If an action cannot be confirmed, follow the retry or refresh message before starting another segment. A test session simulates these actions and does not create a recording file.
