A Bulletin Board discussion lets you read released contributions and reply to other participants. Open the discussion activity from your study. If it is waiting for the research team, select **Check discussion** or return later.

## Reply and submit

Read the released contributions. Under **Choose a comment**, select a comment from another participant. Write under **Your reply**, then select **Send reply for review**. Your unsent reply stays on the page until you send it. Send it before leaving the page.

Depending on the study, you may need to reply to a specified number of different comments or different people. Replies can also be optional. The activity shows its requirement and progress.

## Review and visibility

For example, if the activity asks for replies to two different people, reply to a contribution from each of two other people. Another reply to the first person does not advance a different-people requirement. You cannot reply to your own contribution. A sent reply can count toward the requirement while it awaits review.

Only you and the research team can see your pending or withheld reply. Other participants and authorised client observers see the approved public version after review. When the requirement is met, choose **Submit activity** for a standalone activity. If it is part of a completion section, use **Next** and select **Complete [section name]** on the final screen. Submitting locks any private question answers. You can continue to reply while the discussion remains open and your access is valid.

If your reply cannot be confirmed, follow the displayed retry for that reply. If the discussion is withdrawn or the study closes, contact the research team if you need help.
