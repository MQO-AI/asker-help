An Observer can review a Bulletin Board without changing study data and can send messages in Team Chat. Open the personal Observer link supplied by the research team and keep it private.

## Review the study

1. In **Overview**, review study completion and participant progress. Pseudonyms connect a participant's submitted progress across the study. Choose **Refresh** to update the view.
2. Choose an activity in the study navigation or select **View activity**. Open a participant's entry under **Submitted answers** to read their answers. Use the page controls to see more answers when available.
3. For a discussion activity, review **Released discussion** and approved replies when the research team has released them.

Once the study Flow is published, Observers can see submitted answers from private activities as well as discussion activities. Draft answers, contact details, research-team private records, and pending or withheld replies are not shown. A pseudonym does not guarantee that written answers are anonymous. A closed study may remain available until your access expires, unless access is revoked, the study or project is archived, or relevant records are erased.

## Message the research team

In the Observer workspace, choose **Team Chat** in the study navigation. The chat is shared with assigned Observers and authorised research staff, and participants cannot access it. Staff can open it from the study workspace. Team Chat is available for a published study that is scheduled, open, or closed, subject to access and retention. Enter a message and choose **Send message**. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line. Choose **Load earlier messages** when available.

If sending is not confirmed, use **Try again** for the same message. If your personal link is unavailable, your access may have expired or been revoked. Contact the research team for help.
