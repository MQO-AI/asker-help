A Bulletin Board is a study in which participants complete activities and, where configured, join discussions over time.

{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

## Create a study

1. Open **Bulletin Boards** and choose **Manage Session Groups**. Open the destination group.
2. Choose **All sessions**, then **New session**. Enter a **Session title**, select **Bulletin Board**, and choose **Create session**. The study workspace opens.

If you need a group, choose **New Session Group** and select or create a permitted project. From **Add your first session**, choose **New Session** and create a Bulletin Board. See [create a Session Group](../focus-groups/create-a-session-group).

## Navigate the study

Return to the study from the group's Sessions list. The workspace contains these tools.

- **Overview** for participation and completion.
- **Study flow** for activities, questions, presentation, and the study window.
- **Discussion** for curated contributions and reply review.
- **People** for access and individual progress.
- **Team Chat** for private messages between observers and the research team.
- **Messages** for contact requirements and communications.
- **Data & analysis** and **Retention** for research data and records management.

Use **Study readiness** to return to the current flow, window, and participant-access status. A first study follows this route.

1. [Build the study flow](./build-the-study-flow). Add a private activity with a written question and a later discussion, then save and preview the draft.
2. Set the study window and review [contact requirements](./set-contact-requirements) under **Messages → Contact requirements**. Choose **Save contact requirements** if you make changes. [Enrol people](./manage-study-people), then [publish the flow](./publish-open-and-close). Saving or publishing does not open the study or send invitations.
3. [Review and send invitations](./send-study-email), or use **People → Personal link** for an individual handoff. During the saved window, staff must confirm **Open study** before participants can enter available activities. See [publish, open, and close](./publish-open-and-close).
4. When participants have submitted material, [curate and release the discussion and moderate replies](./curate-and-moderate-discussion).
5. [Monitor participation](./monitor-participation), then [create a snapshot and export results](./snapshot-and-export-study-data).

If the study cannot be created or loaded, check your access and try again.
