An automatic email plan sends eligible follow-ups for a selected Bulletin Board cohort. Prepare it in **Messages > Email reminders** after enrolling people and sending their invitations.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

1. Select up to 100 people who have received an invitation. New enrolments are not added automatically to the selected cohort.
2. Select the triggers you need: **New activity available**, **Unfinished activity reminder**, or **Study closing reminder**. Set hours after availability or before closing where applicable, and write the subject and email for each selected trigger.
3. Set **Minimum hours between automatic emails**. The timing fields use whole hours from 1 to 168 and the study time zone. Confirm permission to send reminders and choose **Save email plan draft**.
4. Choose **Review email plan**. Check each person's eligibility, exact examples, schedule, and maximum message estimate. Resolve blocked recipients before choosing **Activate reviewed email plan**.

For example, select invited participants and configure an unfinished activity reminder. Review the eligible people and example messages before activating the plan.

If review is unavailable, check the wording and timing retained for each rule as well as the selected triggers. Saving a draft does not activate it. The active plan remains in place while a replacement draft is prepared; draft changes take effect after a fresh review and activation. Choose **Pause email reminders** to pause the active plan and cancel its pending automatic emails. Manual invitations remain separate. Email delivery outcomes appear under **Email invitations**.

An existing email suppression blocks delivery to that address. Confirming contact permission does not override it. Eligibility is checked again before delivery, including after a plan has been activated.

If the result is unconfirmed, use **Check the same request**. If people or the saved plan change, refresh and review the current plan before activation.
