Contact requirements determine whether a participant must agree to study texts before starting activities. Before opening the study, open **Messages > Contact requirements**. This setting controls participant entry; it does not send a message.

{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

## Choose a requirement

Choose one of the following:

- **No SMS requirement** keeps participation available without a study-text requirement.
- **SMS required to participate** requires a mobile number and permission for study texts before activities can start. Email does not replace this requirement.

For the SMS requirement, enter the **SMS participation notice** and **Participant support contact**. Explain who may text participants, why, and how to withdraw permission. Use **Preview participant notice** to check what a person will see, then save the requirements. Previewing does not record permission.

## Prepare delivery separately

Before opening the study, choose **Save contact requirements**. Configure and review messages separately. If saving is unconfirmed, choose **Check saved requirements** and review the result before trying again. Once the study opens, its contact requirement cannot be edited.

If a participant cannot or does not want to receive texts, they can use the support contact you provided.
