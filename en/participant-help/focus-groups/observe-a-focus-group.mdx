The observer backroom lets you follow a Focus Group and communicate with the research team. Open your personal observer link from the team. When the backroom is available, choose **Enter observer backroom**. You can meet other observers there while the main session gets ready.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

Your microphone and camera are optional. You can enter listen-only. When you turn them on, people in the backroom can hear or see you, but participants in the main session cannot. When the main session is live, its feed lets you watch and listen to the discussion. Open **Audio and video** and use **Main session** and **Private backroom** to adjust or mute each feed. Headphones can help prevent feedback.

You can read participant chat, but cannot send messages there. Participants, moderators, and observers can read it. Use **Team chat** to communicate with the backroom team. To ask a question, open **Questions**, enter it, and choose **Send question**. Other observers and the team can see questions and replies. Participants cannot see Team chat or observer questions.

If a recording starts, the room shows a notice. Contact the research team if you have questions about the recording or your access.

Choose **Leave** and confirm to exit. If your link or devices do not work, see [Fix joining and device problems](./fix-joining-and-devices).
