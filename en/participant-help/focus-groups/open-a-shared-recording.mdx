A shared link lets you open a Focus Group recording or asset that the sender has made available to you. A recipient-specific delivery from the Sharing Centre requires a password. A direct link to one asset can open without a password, and anyone with that link can open it. Keep your links and password private.

## Open a protected delivery

1. If you have not set a password for recording links shared with you from this Focus Group, open the password setup link in the email. It works once and expires after 48 hours. Create and confirm a password, then choose **Save password**. You can use **Generate password** and **Copy password** to save it before continuing.
2. Choose **Open recording** after setup, or open the recording link from the email. Enter your password and choose **Unlock**. For a return visit, use the recording link and your existing password.
3. Play the available recording. If a transcript was included, you can review it with the recording. If the sender allowed downloads and a download is available, choose **Download**. Images can be viewed on the page, and other available assets may offer **Open [asset name]**.

Use the same password for recipient-specific recording links shared with you from that Focus Group. If you forgot it or the setup link expired before you set it, ask the sender for a new password link. A reset replaces your previous password for your recording links in that Focus Group.

## Open a direct asset link

Open the link from the sender. If it opens without a password, play the video, view the image, or use **Open [asset name]** when offered. Keep the link private because anyone who has it can open it.

If a recording or asset link has expired or been revoked, ask the sender to check the share. If the recording was removed under the study's retention rules, an extended link may not restore it. Ask the sender to check whether it is still available.
