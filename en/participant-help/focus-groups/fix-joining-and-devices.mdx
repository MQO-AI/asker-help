Joining and device checks help you enter a Focus Group and take part with working sound and video. If you cannot join, read the message on the joining page first.

## Before entering

If you are too early, keep the page open or return using the same personal link when the waiting room opens. If the page lists device or screen requirements, use a supported device and screen size, and turn on any required microphone or camera. Contact the research team if you cannot meet a requirement.

If the browser blocked your camera or microphone, allow access in its site permissions. Then check the device selection on the joining page.

- For a microphone, choose **Show microphones** if offered, select the named device, and choose **Test microphone**. Speak and check that the input meter moves. Use **Refresh** if you need to update the device list.
- For a camera, check the selected device and preview. If shown, choose **Retry preview** and check the picture.
- For speakers or headphones, choose **Play test sound**. If you do not hear it, check the volume and selected sound output or your system sound settings.

Observers can enter listen-only when their devices are unavailable. Changing permission alone may not resolve a disconnected or unavailable device.

## Inside the room or returning

Open **Audio and video** in the room to select devices or test your microphone. Mute your session microphone before a private test. If sound is paused, choose **Try audio again** or **Enable audio** if shown. Observers can adjust the separate **Main session** and **Private backroom** controls under **Audio and video**.

If the connection is interrupted, follow the reconnect message. If it asks you to return to the joining page, reopen your personal link and check your access. If another tab is using the same connection, close that tab first.

If your link is expired or revoked, ask the research team for a new active link. If the session has ended or a device problem persists, contact the team and explain which step failed. Keep your personal link private when asking for help.
