Study notifications show updates about your Bulletin Board activities and progress. Open your personal study link and choose **Notifications** in the study header. An unread indicator appears when there are updates to review.

For example, when an update says an activity is ready, choose **Open activity** to go to it, or choose **Mark as read** to mark that update as read without opening the activity. Use **Older notifications** and **Newer notifications** when available. If an earlier update is no longer available, check the study home for current activities. An email or text depends on the study’s communication settings and your contact choice.

## Review text contact

When **Text contact** is available, open it to review the study notice and your choice. To agree, enter the country calling code and international phone number, confirm that the number can receive texts, and choose **Save my choice**. The number you provide has not been verified. You can instead choose not to agree and save that choice.

If the study requires text contact, declining means you need to contact the research team to discuss taking part. Your saved responses are retained. If texts to your number are blocked, contact the research team using the support contact shown. If a save is not confirmed or the notice changes, choose **Load saved contact choice** and review the notice before trying again.
