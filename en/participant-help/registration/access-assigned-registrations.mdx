An assigned Registration gives you access to an organisation’s approved roster or check-in tool. Use your own account and the invitation or sign-in route provided by the administrator.

{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

## Accept a roster invitation

Open the invitation link and check the organisation, invited email, and expiry. If you already have an account, sign in with the invited email, review any **Download Terms**, enter your password, and accept the invitation. If you are signed in with a different account, use the option to switch accounts.

If you are new, enter your name and create a password, review any terms, and create the account. Open the activation link sent to your email, then sign in with the invited address. Complete any requested identity verification before downloading.

## Use an assignment

On **Assigned registrations**, open the assignment to review its sessions, expiry, and included fields. Download the approved roster when available, or choose **Open check-in** if that tool is assigned to you. Your access is limited to the assigned sessions and tools.

If an invitation is expired, unavailable, or already used, follow the page's sign-in or retry guidance. If no active assignment appears, or a download is no longer available, contact the organisation administrator.
