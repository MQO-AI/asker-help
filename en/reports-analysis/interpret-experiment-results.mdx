Experiment results summarise choices and modelled preferences from MaxDiff or conjoint questions.

Research specialists can review them in a standard report when the experiment has sufficient eligible data and results are available. For example, compare attribute importance only within the design and population used for the fitted model.

Open the report and inspect the experiment card. Check the applied report population, weighting, eligible base, and any warnings before interpreting the result. Comparison groups do not refit the experiment model.

MaxDiff results summarise the choices and item scores for the available tasks. Conjoint results can show **Part-worth utilities**, attribute statistics, level appearances, and relative importance. When available, **Holdout validation** reports measures such as top-choice accuracy. Actual-use follow-up results appear only when collected and available.

Use the displayed base and model warnings when communicating results. Part-worths describe relative preferences within the fitted design; they are not direct market shares. Save the report before downloading the experiment's long CSV for the report population. For hypothetical combinations of conjoint levels, see the [aggregate share simulator](./use-conjoint-simulator).
