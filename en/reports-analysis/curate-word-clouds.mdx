A word cloud summarises frequently used words or phrases in written answers for a report.

{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

Report authors can adjust one in the chart settings of a standard report. For example, group common spelling variants of a service name under one display label, then check the result against the written answers and its base.

1. Choose **Phrase length**: **Single words**, **Up to two words**, or **Up to three words**. The initial choice is up to three words.
2. Optionally enter phrases under **Exclude phrases, one per line**, or limit the view with **Only show these phrases, one per line**.
3. Under **Spelling and phrase groups**, add groups manually or choose **AI cleanup** for suggestions. Review each suggested display label and variant before accepting or editing it. AI cleanup uses the chart's filters and needs at least 20 written answers.
4. Choose **Use chart settings**, then **Apply changes** and **Save** the report. Each phrase or combined term counts once per respondent using the selected weights.

The original answers remain unchanged. Phrase grouping helps make a cloud readable; it does not replace a thematic coding review. **Include these words in the published report** starts off. If selected, public results remain subject to the report's minimum-base rules. Check the shared view before sending a link.
