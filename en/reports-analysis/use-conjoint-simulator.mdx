The conjoint share simulator compares hypothetical concepts using a fitted model's aggregate preferences.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

Research specialists can use a conjoint report's **Aggregate share simulator** after a model is available. For example, compare alternative service concepts, changing one level at a time to examine how the scenario result changes.

1. Open the conjoint chart settings and select **Market simulator** under **Report elements**. Choose **Use chart settings**, then **Apply changes**. The simulator is initially off.
2. Name each concept and choose a level for every attribute. Add or remove concepts to compare between two and six.
3. If the model contains a None utility, choose whether to **Include None**. Review each concept's part-worth contributions and the **Scenario result**.
4. Change levels or concepts to explore another scenario. Review the model's applied population, weights, and warnings alongside the result.

The displayed share of preference is a directional aggregate conditional-logit result for the concepts in that scenario. It is not a forecast of market share or respondent-level segmentation. Changing the set of alternatives changes the comparison. Save and share the report using the standard [publication process](./share-standard-report) if it is suitable for the audience.
