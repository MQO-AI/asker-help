Open **Recruitment** in the registration. For example, review permission and eligibility for a person who expressed interest in a study before assigning them to an available session.

## Add and review interest

Choose **Add interest record** to enter a person, or **Import Survey interest** to choose a Survey and published version. For an import, map the interest or contact-permission question and contact fields, state the agreed purpose of follow-up, select responses, and review before importing.

Open **Review** for a person. Check contact details, the agreed purpose, permission evidence, and screening notes. Save the screening decision and selection. To assign a person, first save them as eligible and selected with permission to contact.

## Assign a person

Under **Assign to a session**, choose a registration listing or Bulletin Board. Review any matching existing person and the chosen destination, confirm the review, and book or enrol. Assignment does not send an invitation; review invitations separately in **Messages**.

If a record or match changed, refresh it and review the current details before retrying. Withdraw interest when follow-up should stop.
