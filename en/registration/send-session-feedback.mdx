Session feedback invitations ask attendees to complete a follow-up Survey after a session.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

In the registration, open **Check-in & feedback**, select the session, and choose **Feedback**. Mark attendance first: the reviewed audience includes confirmed registrants marked present. Review the Survey and the people included before sending.

1. Choose a **Feedback survey**. Enter sender name and reply-to email, then write the English and, when needed, French subject and message.
2. Choose **Save setup**, then **Review recipients & email**. Inspect the Survey, recipient list, and message in each language. Resolve any review errors.
3. Confirm that you reviewed the recipients, Survey, and email, then choose the **Send feedback** action.

The session feedback link is added to the message and remains available for 14 days after sending. Answers are associated with the session, not individual registrations. Individual completion is not tracked. Check **Messages** for delivery status; a queued invitation does not mean it has been read.

If setup or review changes, save and review the current version before sending. If an error appears, follow the message and check the status before trying again.
