Information requests send selected registrants a separate personal form for follow-up details.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

Open **Registrants** and select eligible people, then choose **Request additional information**. You can also open **Messages > Information requests**. For example, request accessibility requirements from selected participants, keeping their individual details out of the email itself.

1. Enter a staff-only request name and response deadline. Search or filter by session, booking status, or registration answer, and select recipients. Changing filters keeps your selection.
2. In **Questions**, write the form title, introduction, and questions. The ASL/CART starter replaces the questions currently in the editor. Prepare English and Canadian French wording as needed.
3. In **Email**, enter sender name, reply-to, subject, and body. A personal form link is added automatically. Keep individual accessibility details out of the email.
4. Choose **Save draft** to retain the work without sending. When ready, choose **Review before sending**, inspect the exact recipient list, deadline, email, and participant form, and resolve any errors. Choose the final **Send request** action.

Cancelled, declined, and expired registrations cannot receive the request. The review identifies selected people who are no longer eligible. Track replies from **Information requests**. If the audience or content changes, review it again before sending.
