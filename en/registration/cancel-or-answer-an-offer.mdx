Manage your booking or waitlist offer through the personal link sent by the organiser.

{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

Open the link to see your registration or offer. Keep the link private because it gives access to your booking.

## Cancel a registration or leave a waitlist

Open the management link and review the session and current status. If cancellation is available, choose **Cancel**, read the consequence, and confirm. You can choose to keep the registration instead. The page confirms a successful cancellation.

## Answer an offer

Open the offer link and check the session and **Respond by** deadline. Choose **Accept place** or **Decline**. The page refreshes to show the result. An expired or closed offer can no longer be answered.

If a link is unavailable, the cancellation window has closed, or an action fails, contact the organiser. Refresh the page before retrying so you can see the current status.
