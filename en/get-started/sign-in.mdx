Signing in connects your account to the Asker work your organisation has made available to you. Use your authorised account to open Asker or return to a link someone has shared with you. Your organisation must have Asker access, and your account must be associated with it. The work and actions available to you also depend on your permissions.

## Sign in

1. Open Asker or the link to the work you need.
2. Follow the sign-in screen. Depending on your organisation's configuration, you may be taken to the shared MQO sign-in or asked for an email address and password in Asker. Complete any security verification requested.
3. When sign-in succeeds, return to the requested page. If you opened Asker directly, use the navigation to find your work.

If the sign-in form offers **Stay logged in**, select it only on a device you trust. The shared sign-in may offer **Account security** for managing security settings.

## Sign out or get back in

Choose **Sign out** in the header when you have finished, especially on a shared device. On a narrow screen, open the navigation menu to find it. If sign-out fails, follow the on-screen message and try again. If you cannot see your organisation or the work you expect, check that you signed in to the correct account and contact your organisation administrator if you need access. If your session expires while you work, keep the original tab open. Use **Sign in in another tab**, return to the original tab, and choose **Retry connection** if needed. Sign in with the same account and organisation. Check your work and whether an interrupted save or download completed before retrying it.
