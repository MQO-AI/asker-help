Access checks determine which organisation, work, and actions your account can open in Asker. Use this guide when you can sign in but cannot reach an expected organisation, page, or control. Sign in using the account that your organisation uses for Asker.

## Choose the next step

- **No organisation:** On **Set up your organization**, select **Continue to MQO** if offered, then create an organisation or accept your team's invitation. Otherwise open your MQO account to complete setup. Contact your organisation administrator if you need an invitation. Signing in alone does not create access to an existing organisation's work.
- **Asker access unavailable:** Contact your organisation administrator or MQO contact to check access for the organisation and your account. Once access is enabled, sign in again and retry the link.
- **Assigned registrations only:** If your account has restricted Registration access, use the assigned registrations page and open the item assigned to you. See [Access assigned registrations](../participant-help/registration/access-assigned-registrations).
- **One item or action is unavailable:** Confirm you are in the right account and organisation, and ask the person responsible for that work to check your assignment or permissions. Access to Asker does not automatically grant every action.

If you are interrupted while editing, keep the original tab open and follow [session recovery](./recover-your-session). Share the name of the item and the error message with your administrator, but do not send your password or security codes.
