Asker permissions determine which areas, projects, studies, and actions your account can use. Check this guide when a colleague can see an item or control that you cannot. Access is checked at several levels. Being able to sign in does not by itself make every project, study, or action available.

{/* Available with */}
{/* Let Q-bit do it */}

- Your account must belong to an organisation that can access Asker.
- Project actions such as creating or archiving a project depend on the permissions available to your account.
- Registration access can be restricted to assigned registrations. Export and sharing actions can have separate permissions.
- Sessions access is checked separately. The ability to use one area does not guarantee access to every session or action.

Controls and navigation may therefore differ between colleagues. If a button or item is missing, confirm the organisation and project, then ask the person responsible for access to check your account and the action you need. Do not assume that a role name alone grants all actions.

If your account is limited to assigned Registrations, follow [Access assigned registrations](../participant-help/registration/access-assigned-registrations). For missing organisation access, see [Resolve unavailable access](./resolve-unavailable-access).
